# Operating model and transfer

The architecture is not a product that gets delivered; it is a capability implanted in the tenant's own infrastructure and transferred. Everything the use-case builder produces lands in the tenant's repositories, in open formats, from day one.

- **Co-delivery with progressive ownership.** Business owns prioritisation and validation; IT co-owns the platform from day one and takes it at handover; security is engaged in week one and co-approves guardrails and access; risk and compliance co-own governance. The builder's records are the design document (the "TADD") that this co-delivery reviews.
- **RACI per use case.** Agent design: delivery responsible, tenant accountable. Operation and review: reviewer responsible, business owner accountable. Integration: IT responsible, tenant accountable. Risk and audit: compliance accountable. After handover the tenant holds every role.
- **Severities.** S1 workflow down or unsafe action; S2 critical action blocked; S3 non-critical defect; S4 service request. S1 carries on-call. Triage follows the layer the trace points at: model or prompt reproduced against eval sets, tool failures isolated with retries, data issues traced to source, infrastructure rebuilt from git, judgement calls escalated to a human gate.
- **The three months map to the stages.** Month 1 (sandbox, access, data pipelines, design document) is Crawl; month 2 (knowledge, alpha agents in non-production, wiring into live processes, UAT) is Walk; month 3 (production cutover, guardrails, co-ownership, handover certification) is Run. The assumptions behind them (access within five business days, a CISO available, SMEs two to three hours a week) are risks the business record names.
- **Where adoption fails.** Data readiness, Infosec approval before the first deployment, and change management: freeing experts for feedback and building trust. Plan for the three explicitly.
- **Shadow agents.** An approved sandbox with identity-based access is where experimentation happens; only the governed pipeline reaches production. The ephemeral runner and GitOps are that structure.
- **Governance playbook.** New agents and autonomy increases are approved together, starting read-only; new tools and data sources are access grants with security review; prompt and configuration changes are versioned and peer-reviewed like code; an oversight committee reviews exceptions. The playbook is a handover deliverable.
